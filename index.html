Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. About the app

Tudo Shop Metricas ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Tudo Shop. It connects to TikTok through the official TikTok APIs (Login Kit and Display API) to read basic profile information and performance metrics of videos posted by Tudo Shop's own TikTok account.

2. Who may use it

The app is for internal use by the Tudo Shop team only. It is not offered to the general public and is not sold or distributed to third parties.

3. What the app does

  • Reads profile information (display name, avatar, follower count, following count, total likes and video count).
  • Reads the list of the account's own public videos and their metrics (views, likes, comments and shares).
  • Shows this data so the team can analyze content performance.

The app does not post, edit, delete or interact with any content, and does not access other users' accounts.

4. Authorization

Access happens only after the account owner logs in with TikTok and approves the requested permissions. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.

5. TikTok's terms

Use of the app is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok for Developers policies. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok.

6. No warranty

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Data we access

With the account owner's authorization, the app accesses only data from Tudo Shop's own TikTok account:

  • Basic profile information: open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic).
  • Profile statistics: follower count, following count, likes count and video count (scope user.info.stats).
  • Public videos and their metrics: title, description, creation date, cover image, views, likes, comments and shares (scope video.list).

2. How we use it

The data is used only to analyze the performance of Tudo Shop's own content and plan future posts. It is not used for advertising profiles, not sold and not shared with third parties.

3. Data of other people

The app does not collect personal data from viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and metrics are stored only on Tudo Shop's own computer, are not published, and are kept only as long as needed for the analysis. Credentials are never shared.

5. Your choices

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions. On request, all stored data is deleted.
